Signal Aurora benchmark

Acceptance requires DPR 2. The protocol alternates AB/BA order, times performance.now() directly around renderer.draw() submission, and reports max(0, auraP95 − baselineP95). Aura states are prebuilt before timing; each twelve-pair block yields before timing and alternates its first arm. Forced one-pixel readback was removed because it dominated and reversed the workload (about 246 ms at 1,300 nodes versus 37 ms at 3,500) instead of measuring frame submission.
